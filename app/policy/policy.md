P-1 | PAN is mandatory for every loan applicant and must follow the format AAAAA9999A.
P-2 | Applicant age must be at least 21 years and at most 60 years at the time of application.
P-3 | Net monthly income must be at least INR 25000 for eligibility.
P-4 | Average monthly bank balance must be at least INR 10000 over the last three months.
P-5 | Applicant name must match across all submitted documents with similarity of at least 85 percent.
P-6 | Required documents are identity proof, income proof and bank statement.
